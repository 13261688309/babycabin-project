What changed
- Real-time MJPEG, the existing model, vision metadata uplink, native USB,
  voice/STOP telemetry and environmental telemetry remain available.
- --snapshot-fallback-url explicitly permits a switch from a failed MJPEG
  connection to repeated same-camera JPEG requests. Default: disabled.
- The old capture worker closes before the fallback opens. There is one
  camera reader, not simultaneous stream and capture clients.
- --network-reader http-snapshot can test a JPEG endpoint directly without
  repeating the known failing long-stream test.
- Each JPEG is bounded to 2MB, checked and decoded. Timeouts, partial JPEGs,
  invalid replies and exhausted retry budgets cannot become valid frames.
- Request-start time is included in frame age. It is not a sensor exposure
  timestamp. Existing 1500ms observation/uplink freshness limits are unchanged.
- The dashboard identifies the single-JPEG fallback. Source failure still
  expires the picture and classification; independent MCU data stays visible.
- JPEGs stay in RAM. No camera settings, firmware, Wi-Fi or model weights change.

Limits and remaining project work
- /capture was observed on actual AI_V1.5.2 for the bounded session above;
  that does not prove indefinite availability or recovery from all outages.
- Both endpoints may fail if the camera or network itself stops responding.
  This feature does not establish or repair that hardware/network root cause.
- No claim of camera stability, recognition accuracy or full-system acceptance.
- Motor, pan/tilt and physical STOP/ARM/PG/watchdog acceptance remain required.
- No actuator power, procurement, fabrication or new camera flash is authorized.
- Voice TX1 must be removed before either the voice board or MCU loses power.
